User guide

Community, knowledge and contributions

Learn how to take part in OSAP and discover how the project aims to connect with the music community.

1. Contact and corrections

La página Contact and corrections lets you send the team a message or report a catalogue problem. If you opened it from a specific work, the request may be associated with that context automatically.

Describe the problem clearly. To correct a title, provide the displayed title and the one you believe is correct. For an authorship issue, provide a reliable reference, edition or source page that verifies it. Do not include passwords or unnecessary personal data.

Requests remain pending review; submitting them does not automatically change catalogue data.

2. Suggest a source

If you know of a music repository that is not listed, you can suggest it from Sources when the form is available. Include its name, type and web address, and briefly explain what musical material it offers and why it would be useful to OSAP.

3. Friends: connecting with the music world

La sección Friends reflects OSAP’s aim of connecting with the wider musical world: ensembles, creators and spaces that share musical information and activities. It is not just a resource list, but a meeting point for communities and music-related content.

Choirs

Any choir can request to be included. Its administrator can maintain the profile and add related notes or material, including YouTube videos and other useful links. Before publishing material, make sure you have the right to share it and that the links are appropriate.

Articles

Registered users can write articles about music-related topics. Keep the content original and useful to the community, and identify references and third-party material correctly.

Upcoming performances

This section allows certain administrators to publish announcements of upcoming performances. Publishing is not necessarily available to every registered account.

4. Contributors

La sección Contributors explains ways to participate and displays acknowledgements where they exist and publication has been authorised. Public visibility of a name depends on the applicable settings and permissions; not all account activity is necessarily public.

Apoyo económico

Help sustain the project’s infrastructure and continuity.

Contenido y revisión

Help with data, reviews, corrections, translations or documentation.

Difusión

5. My activity

Once signed in, the section My activity may show downloads, contributions, resources and statistics associated with your account. Ongoing operations and materialised items depend on activity recorded by the system.

To help the review, always provide any evidence you have: source link, alternative title, catalogue reference or a clear explanation. This helps the team assess the request more accurately.
