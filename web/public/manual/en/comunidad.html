Friends and music community

OSAP aims to connect with ensembles, creators and spaces that share musical knowledge and activity.

Contact and corrections

Describe the issue and provide a reliable reference. Submitting a request does not automatically change catalogue data.

Choirs

Any choir can participate. Choir administrators can edit the profile and attach scores, materials and links, including YouTube videos.

Articles and performances

Registered users can write music-related articles. Authorised administrators can publish announcements of upcoming performances.
