Friends and music community

OSAP aims to connect with ensembles, creators and spaces that share musical knowledge and activity.

Use Contact and corrections to describe an issue and provide a reliable reference. Any choir can participate; choir administrators can edit profiles and attach scores, materials and links including YouTube. Registered users can write music-related articles, and authorised administrators can announce upcoming performances.
