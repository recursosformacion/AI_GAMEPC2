How to use OpenMusicRepository

This guide explains how to find scores and musical resources, explore composers and works, understand available sources and take part in the project.

One important point: OSAP brings together information about works, composers and versions from different catalogues. It does not necessarily host the original file; in many cases, it directs you to the source that provides it.

How to read this guide

Instructions are organised by task. Some options depend on your session, account permissions and connected sources. If an option does not appear, it may not be enabled for your account or may have changed in the deployed version.

Resource availability: OSAP helps locate and connect musical information. Download availability, licensing and access to the original file depend on the relevant provider.
