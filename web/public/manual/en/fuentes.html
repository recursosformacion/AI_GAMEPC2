Sources, providers and formats

Understand where information comes from, what formats mean and how to open material from its source.

1. What is a source?

A source is a catalogue, repository or external service from which OSAP obtains information about works and resources. OSAP brings together and links data from different sources so you can search in one place, but it does not replace the original repository.

The catalogue lists available sources, information about their capabilities, documentation and the formats they say they provide. Connection status may change over time.

2. Main music sources

OSAP brings together references from different catalogues and repositories so you can find works and their representations through a single search. Each source has its own scope, terms of use and technical capabilities. A work appearing in OSAP does not mean all its files are hosted here or can be opened directly in the viewer.

What does “direct access” mean in OSAP?

In OSAP, distinguish three situations: an **external reference**, where the record links to the provider’s page (such as IMSLP); a **reference with no file available in OSAP**, as is currently the case with CPDL; and an **integrated OSAP repository**, such as OMR, accessed through catalogue representations. Even in an integrated repository, the viewer appears only if the file is accessible and its format can be converted to a supported format.

Important: «gratuito» no significa «sin derechos». Antes de copiar, modificar, distribuir o publicar una partitura, revisa la licencia de esa edición y la normativa aplicable en tu país.

3. Common formats

Sibelius, Finale, Guitar Pro and other application formatsNative files or files specific to an application.Edit with compatible software; readability depends on the format and version.

4. Opening or downloading a resource

On a record, find the representation you want and check its provider and format. Use the available action to view, download or open the source. If the resource does not open, the provider may be temporarily unavailable, the file may have been removed or an external account may be required.

When you leave OSAP to visit the source, that site’s terms apply. Check its licence and usage restrictions before redistributing or publishing a file.

5. Source catalogue and connected sources

El Source catalogue presenta información más detallada de cada proveedor, como sus capacidades, documentación, formatos y catálogos. La sección Sources muestra fuentes conocidas o conectadas y puede ofrecer un formulario para sugerir una nueva.

If you suggest a source, use its recognisable name and a stable web address. If the form asks for a type or location, provide the repository type and the URL of its official page, API or identifying manifest. Accepting a suggestion does not automatically activate technical integration.

Availability and licences: que una referencia aparezca en OSAP no garantiza que el archivo se pueda descargar gratis ni que esté en dominio público. Comprueba las condiciones indicadas por la fuente original.
