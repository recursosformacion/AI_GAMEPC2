Sources, providers and formats

Understand where information comes from, what file formats mean and how to access material at its source.

Understand where information comes from, what file formats mean and how to access material at its source.

What is a source?

OSAP links information from catalogues and repositories, but does not necessarily host the original file.

Main music sources

IMSLP offers scores and editions; conditions vary by file and country. Mutopia provides free downloads under stated licences. CPDL/ChoralWiki offers free choral scores on its own website. OMR is OSAP’s own repository and incorporates PDMX data; availability and rights vary by resource.

Formats and access

MusicXML/MXL may enable musical viewing when the file is accessible and compatible. PDFs and other formats may require opening the original source.
