Sources and formats

Understand where information comes from, what file formats mean and how to access material at its source.

IMSLP offers scores and editions; conditions vary by file and country. Mutopia provides free downloads under stated licences. CPDL/ChoralWiki offers free choral scores on its own website. OMR is OSAP’s own repository and incorporates PDMX data; availability and rights vary by resource. MusicXML/MXL may enable musical viewing when accessible and compatible.
