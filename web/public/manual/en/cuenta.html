User guide

Account, access and personal activity

Learn to sign in, create an account, manage your display name and view available personal history.

1. Browsing without signing in

Many catalogue pages can be explored without an account. Some actions, such as viewing personal activity or using features reserved for signed-in users, require signing in.

2. Creating an account and signing in

Utiliza el botón Sign in de la cabecera. OSAP intenta abrir el flujo de autenticación configurado; si no puede hacerlo, puede mostrar un formulario alternativo para iniciar sesión o registrarte. Sigue las instrucciones que aparezcan en pantalla y utiliza una dirección de correo a la que tengas acceso.

If you cannot sign in, check your details and make sure the authentication service is available. Do not share your credentials with support.

3. Account name and signing out

When signed in, the header shows the name or email associated with your account. Selecting it lets you edit the name if the service supports this. The account name and permission to appear publicly as a contributor are not necessarily the same.

Usa Sign out cuando termines en un dispositivo compartido. La aplicación también contempla el cierre de sesión automático tras un periodo prolongado de inactividad.

4. My activity

En el menú My activity puedes consultar la información personal que el sistema haya registrado, como:

  • Downloads and quota usage.
  • Access stage and remaining allowance, where applicable.
  • Contributions and review status.
  • Works, representations, resources or files associated with materialised contributions.

The page may be empty if no activity has been recorded yet or an operation has not completed. A pending request is not the same as an accepted contribution.

5. Language and appearance

The header includes a language selector and a light/dark theme control. Language settings affect translated interface text. Work titles, proper names, provider data and some external content may remain in their original language.

6. Quotas and download access

Download access may depend on the account’s access stage, daily limits and current rules. If you reach a limit, read the message shown by the application. Terms may vary, and quota administration tools are not available to ordinary users.

Privacy: no compartas enlaces de sesión, tokens, contraseñas ni información de pago. Consulta las páginas de privacidad y cookies de OSAP para conocer las condiciones aplicables.

También puedes consultar el privacy notice y la cookie policy.
